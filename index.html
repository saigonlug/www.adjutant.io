Case 03 / Logistics platform

Positive cash flow could absorb the cost of outages. Eroded customer trust was harder to repair.

Revenue doubled each year, but the system crashed roughly every two weeks.

Operations absorbed the disruption; reporting relied on manual extracts and Excel.

What our consultant helped change

Plan focused services

Separate driver, customer, admin, fleet, and warehouse responsibilities.

Build the data platform

Data lake → warehouse → real-time analytics, separate from the operational system.

Replace manual reporting

Connect current operational data for accurate, timely business insight.

What this enabled

More dependable operations. Live business insight.

Contain disruption, improve service, and support informed decisions as the business grows.
